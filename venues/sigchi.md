# Venue variant: ACM CHI / SIGCHI paper submission

For reviewing a paper submitted to CHI (or another SIGCHI venue — adjust the
venue name below). This variant replaces exactly two pieces of
`REVIEW-INSTRUCTIONS.md`: the shared calibration and the PF seat lens. Every
other seat (R1–R5, R4 Devil's Advocate, the wildcard) runs unchanged, as do all
process rules (§3 checkpoint, §4b purity, §5 output, §8 logs, §11 wildcard).
Like the frozen prompts, this variant is edited only here, in git, never inline
for a run.

## Target (replaces §2 "Solicitation")

ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (or the SIGCHI venue
named in the checkpoint). The panel evaluates the manuscript as a CHI
submission against the venue's review criteria: significance of the
contribution, validity and rigor of the methods, situation within HCI
literature, and clarity of the writing.

## Shared calibration (replaces the §4c shared calibration; seat lenses append to THIS text)

> Read the WHOLE extracted text at: `<PATH>` (page markers `=== PAGE N ===`). It
> is a paper submitted to ACM CHI. Judge what is on the page.
> This is a CHI PAPER SUBMISSION, not a grant proposal. Evaluate against CHI
> review criteria: (1) significance and originality of the contribution to HCI;
> (2) validity and rigor of the methods for the claims made; (3) situation
> within and engagement with prior HCI literature; (4) clarity and quality of
> the writing and figures; (5) fit and interest for the CHI audience. Consider
> the paper category and length norms (full paper vs. short paper) when judging
> scope.
> DRAFT MODE: focus on framing, structure, and scientific substance. Internal
> notes, TODOs, typos, and formatting get one brief pre-submission-checklist
> mention — they must not dominate severity or the verdict.
> You review independently; you see no other reviewer's comments. Every
> substantive claim cites a page. Genuine strengths acknowledged; no
> manufactured balance. Every weakness: what's wrong, where, and a concrete fix.
> Define each technical term at first use for a reader newer to experimental
> methods. READ-ONLY on all files. English. Your final message IS the report —
> raw markdown, no preamble, ~600-900 words.
> FORMAT: `## <SEAT> — <Name>` — where `<Name>` is the seat's role title, never
> an invented persona name. / **Persona:** (1 line) / **Recommendation:**
> (CHI scale: Strong Accept / Accept / Neutral / Reject / Strong Reject, with
> an expertise self-rating) / `### Overall verdict` (short paragraph) /
> `### Strengths` (bullets, page cites) / `### Weaknesses` (numbered; severity
> CRITICAL/MAJOR/MINOR; page; fix) / `### Pre-submission checklist` (brief
> bullets) / `### Bottom line` (2-3 sentences).

## PF seat (replaces the §4c PF lens)

> **PF — Venue Fit & Contribution.** Senior HCI researcher; veteran CHI
> Associate Chair. Lens: whether the stated contribution is a CHI contribution
> and is claimed at the right size; fit and interest for the CHI audience;
> whether the abstract, introduction, and conclusion promise and deliver the
> same paper; completeness against CHI submission expectations (contribution
> statement, ethics and consent reporting, limitations section, accessibility
> of figures); competitiveness versus typical accepted CHI papers in this area.
